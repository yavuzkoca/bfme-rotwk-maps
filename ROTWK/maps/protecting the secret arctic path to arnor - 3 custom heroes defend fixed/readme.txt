Hi, my Alias is PowerOfJustice in BFME II (TM) Rise of The Witch King and this is my 3 custom
hero map named The Sacred Path to Arnor.

Players : 3

Size : Small

Map Kind : CaH Survival Map

You have 3 lives in total when all of your heroes and your allies heroes have died the map is
lost, if your hero would die at any point don't despair, for at wave 3 and wave 6 reinforcement
will appear with a hero for player 1 at wave 3 and a new hero for player 3 at wave 6, player 2 
starts with Faramir at the forbidden pool, scroll around you.

Known Bugs : at wave 6 or 7 a thrallmaster will get stuck at a mountain, due to limited area 
to stand on, so if your noticing that the next wave isnt spawning and all mobs are dead.
it's most likely this.

This is my first CaH map and it has been in so many version I dont dare to think how many.
This is the least buggy one and as far as I know there's only 1 bug. I have tested it ALOT!

Enjoy my map and please do post comments or bugs to my mail at : Battlefield_13@hotmail.com!
All help can improve this map and future maps.

Installment : start up Run.exe or Run on the Start menu, go to  C:/Documents and Settings/*Username*/Application Data/
and look for your BFME2 maps folder their.
NOTE: This is for Windows Xp, I do not know the way to install it on Vista or Windows 7 sorry.
